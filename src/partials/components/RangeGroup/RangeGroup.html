Native reference

Two plain <input type="number"> bounding the same span: zero JavaScript, no overlap, no clamping strategy, no aria-valuetext — and the value can be typed. When the span must be exact, or is mostly filled in by keyboard, this is the better answer, and it is worth asking why a slider was wanted before building the three behaviours above.
